# Changelog

## 0.2.1-dm.21 (demios78 fork)

- Coffee tables can have a brick baseplate top (blue plates, a green plate, a train track loop).

## 0.2.1-dm.20 (demios78 fork)

- New furniture: a clear drawer tower (3 x 12 drawers full of bricks) with an oak top.

## 0.2.1-dm.19 (demios78 fork)

- New decor: a minifigure wall clock that shows the real time.

## 0.2.1-dm.18 (demios78 fork)

- New light: a studio softbox on a tripod, standing on the floor or hung from the ceiling.

## 0.2.1-dm.17 (demios78 fork)

- Stairwell railings stop where a wall begins.

## 0.2.1-dm.16 (demios78 fork)

- Stairs can have an open well beside them; railings follow the opening and skip edges under an upper flight.

## 0.2.1-dm.15 (demios78 fork)

- Sliding closet doors can have colored (frosted or mirrored) panels.

## 0.2.1-dm.14 (demios78 fork)

- Doors can be a glass door with fixed floor-length side panels.
- Double-hung windows 2 m or wider show as a set of side-by-side windows.

## 0.2.1-dm.13 (demios78 fork)

- Doors can be open doorways: a cased opening without a door, 0.6-3 m wide.

## 0.2.1-dm.12 (demios78 fork)

- Windows can be double-hung (wooden sashes with a grid), optionally with black shutters outside.
- New window covering: pleated two-tone drapes down to the floor.

## 0.2.1-dm.11 (demios78 fork)

- Doors can be French doors: a pair of white glazed leaves (2 x 5 panes each), 1-3 m wide.

## 0.2.1-dm.10 (demios78 fork)

- Doors can be sliding closet doors: two white bypass panels, 1-3 m wide, no swing area.

## 0.2.1-dm.9 (demios78 fork)

- Stairs can be closed underneath (a closet under the stairs): basement stairs below them get no railing on the
  floor above. A door may open onto stairs, such as a basement door.

## 0.2.1-dm.8 (demios78 fork)

- Brick models light up while their bound switch or light is on (engines, cockpits, the Concorde's landing and
  wingtip lights, bulbs along the coaster track), and can hang on the wall on a floating shelf.

## 0.2.1-dm.7 (demios78 fork)

- New decor: brick display models (Millennium Falcon, X-wing, TIE fighter, U-wing, N-1 starfighter, Helicarrier,
  Concorde and a loop coaster), each at its real size; pick the model in the card.

## 0.2.1-dm.6 (demios78 fork)

- New furniture: a carved shutter mirror for the wall, a fold-top writing desk, a three-drawer nightstand and a
  standing cheval mirror; the table lamp gets a wood frame model. Colors can be chosen.

## 0.2.1-dm.5 (demios78 fork)

- New furniture: a storage bench with carved doors that open and a seat cushion, a wicker wing chair and an ironing
  board (bind the iron's plug and its light glows while on). Colors can be chosen.

## 0.2.1-dm.4 (demios78 fork)

- New furniture: a loveseat (striped or plain fabric), a wicker armchair with a floral or plain cushion, a scalloped
  shell chair and an upholstered task chair. The shell and task chairs tuck under desks. Colors can be chosen.

## 0.2.1-dm.3 (demios78 fork)

- New furniture: a daybed with three drawers that pulls out into a king. Choose Twin or King in its card; the back
  stays against the wall. Frame and bedding colors can be chosen.

## 0.2.1-dm.2 (demios78 fork)

- New furniture: a twin loft bed with a ladder on either side, a guard rail and open space below. Frame and bedding
  colors can be chosen.

## 0.2.1-dm.1 (demios78 fork)

- Beds come in Twin, Full, Queen and King sizes, with five headboard styles: flat, framed panels, padded, arched and
  carved lattice. Frame and bedding colors can be chosen, including a floral print.
- Nightstands can have a carved lattice door and a color of their own.
- New furniture: a narrow console table with an adjustable length.
- Installs from github.com/demios78/homescape.

## 0.2.1

- The average temperature counts only indoor rooms: terraces, balconies and gardens no longer change it
  ([#3](https://github.com/muratozturkdev/homescape/issues/3)).
- LED strips have their own length, set in their card; the strip, its glow and its light follow it, and it never gets
  longer than its wall ([#4](https://github.com/muratozturkdev/homescape/issues/4)).
- Installing or updating the app builds exactly this version.

## 0.2.0

- Open-plan rooms: walls can be left out ([#2](https://github.com/muratozturkdev/homescape/issues/2)). In edit mode click
  a wall to select and delete it, or use the new Walls list in the room card, where a room can also lose all its walls.
  The rooms keep their own name, Home Assistant area and stats; lamps light the whole open space, and walk mode, people
  and pets pass through. Balcony and terrace railings can be removed the same way.
- Room names can be hidden in the 3D view (the tag button in the tool column, on phones in the three-dot menu).
- The sample home's names follow the language.
- The top bar fits small phones: under 640 px fullscreen moves into the three-dot menu.
- Plans are saved in a new format (6). Homescape 0.1.0 never writes over them: it stops syncing until it is updated.

## 0.1.0

- First release: Homescape in the Home Assistant sidebar through ingress.
